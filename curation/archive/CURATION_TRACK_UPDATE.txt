Supersedes the previous update. Three items, one of which corrects something
this track relied on.

=== 1. CORRECTION: THE AHCA FILE YOU MATCHED AGAINST IS 18 MONTHS STALE ===

The BA_Providers file in the project was AHCA's February 2025 list. I have
replaced it with the current one, BA_Providers_August_2026.xlsx, downloaded
today from AHCA's Recipient Support page.

The current list has 387 providers. 378 distinct names, 381 distinct phones,
43 counties.

Your response cited 1,473 providers and reported 227 CONFIRMED matches at
14.5%. That was measured against a list roughly four times larger than the
one AHCA publishes today. Those 227 confirmations need re-checking against
the current file before they are cited again.

Two things also became clear reading the file's own disclaimer:

  - It covers FEE-FOR-SERVICE only. The file explicitly tells families
    enrolled in a Medicaid managed care plan to contact their plan for
    participating providers instead. Most Florida Medicaid recipients are in
    SMMC managed care, so the majority of Medicaid BA providers are not on
    this list at all.
  - It is partly opt-in. Certified analysts email AHCA to be added to or
    removed from it.

So it is a much weaker whitelist than either of us assumed. A match still
confirms. A non-match proves even less than we thought. Worth re-running the
cross-match against the new file, but do not treat the result as a coverage
measure.
