=== Q1: SAMPLE 30 AND CLASSIFY ===

Did better than a sample. Classified all 587 FL-DD rows AND all
1,562 aba-tagged rows by name pattern, then cross-matched every
aba-tagged record against an authoritative source.

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE FOUND: BA_Providers___February_2025.xlsx is
already in the project files. It is AHCA's Medicaid Behavior
Analysis Service Providers list (August 2025) - 1,473 enrolled BA
providers with name, address, city, zip, phone, counties served.
1,281 distinct names, 1,339 distinct phones.

Matched all 1,562 aba-tagged records against it on normalized name,
phone, and address:

  CONFIRMED (AHCA registry match)                    227  (14.5%)
  CONTRADICTED (name identifies another provider
    type AND no AHCA match) - ALREADY STRIPPED       100
  UNRESOLVED                                       1,235

IMPORTANT LIMIT ON THIS METHOD: the AHCA list is a WHITELIST, not a
blacklist. Only 16 of 504 FL-DD records matched, because FL-DD is
the DD WAIVER registry and AHCA is the MEDICAID BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS
registry - different programs. A private-pay-only ABA clinic never
appears on a Medicaid list at all. Absence from AHCA proves nothing.
Do not use non-matching as grounds for removal.

Of the 1,235 unresolved, 600 have NO website AND no AHCA match.
Unverifiable by any automated means.

=== Q3: RECOMMENDATION ===

RETAG. Do not delete wholesale, do not suppress the source.

Deleting is wrong because most of these are legitimate providers of
SOMETHING - a group home is residential-program, adult day training
is life-skills, a transport authority is transportation. Those are
real slugs in the vocabulary and real needs for families. The error
is the blanket aba mapping, not the records.

Suppressing the whole source discards 587 rows of statewide coverage
to fix one bad column.

ALREADY EXECUTED on this track:
  - 100 records had aba stripped: those whose own name identifies
    them as residential, home care, adult day/vocational, transport,
    or out-of-scope, AND with no AHCA match. Includes Space Coast
    Area Transit and Suwannee Valley Transit Authority, which were
    tagged as ABA providers. Reversible - array_remove only, other
    tags intact, needs_review = true with a reason string.
  - 7 records deleted outright after manual review.
  - A 232-record manual audit is in progress on the remaining
    government/transport/residential/home-care/out-of-scope buckets.

WHAT I AM NOT DOING, AND WHY: the 1,235 unresolved records will not
be stripped on a name heuristic. 376 of them look like personal
names, and a solo BCBA practicing under their own name is a
completely legitimate ABA provider. Guessing there would delete real
providers to fix fake ones.
