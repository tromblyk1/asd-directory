Mapping guidance for these provider types:
  group home / assisted living / adult family care -> residential-program
  adult day training / vocational / supported living -> life-skills

  in-home or community-based service delivery -> mobile-services

service dog training. A service dog trainer places a trained task
animal with an individual handler and is NOT animal-therapy. If a
record is a service dog trainer, leave services_proposed empty and
